## Purpose

This declaration is required to establish the basis on which the artist (or the entity they represent) holds rights in the sound recordings subject to the MusiGod neighboring rights audit. SoundExchange distributes the copyright owner / master rightsholder share to the entity or person that owns or controls the master recording. MusiGod requires this documentation to identify to whom neighboring rights royalties should flow and to determine the correct scope of the audit.

**Why this matters:** SoundExchange pays the copyright-owner share to the entity holding master rights — not necessarily to the performing artist. If the master is owned by a label, production company, or other entity, SoundExchange distributions may be (or should be) flowing to that entity rather than to the performing artist directly. MusiGod must document the ownership structure before making any representations about uncollected royalties.
