## Purpose

This declaration is required to confirm the artist's role as a featured performer on the sound recordings in their catalog. MusiGod uses this declaration to determine eligibility for neighboring rights audit services and to document the basis for any neighboring rights recovery claim.

**Why this matters:** SoundExchange distributes sound recording performance royalties separately to (a) copyright owners / master rightsholders and (b) featured recording artists. An artist must qualify as a "featured performer" to receive the featured performer share. Non-featured musicians (session players, background vocalists not prominently featured on the label) receive a separate non-featured share distributed through AFM and SAG-AFTRA. This declaration establishes the artist's featured performer status.
