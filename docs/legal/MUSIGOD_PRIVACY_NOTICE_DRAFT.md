### What We Collect

When you go through the MusiGod intake process, we collect:

| What | Why |
|------|-----|
| Your legal name, stage name, and performing aliases | To identify you correctly in the audit and in any communications with collection organizations |
| Your email address | To send you intake updates, reminders, and your audit findings |
| Your performer role, PRO affiliations, and SoundExchange membership status | To determine your eligibility for neighboring rights recovery services |
| Documents you upload (SoundExchange statements, distributor statements, ownership evidence) | To conduct your neighboring rights audit |
| Metadata about those documents (file name, size, type, content fingerprint) | To track your intake checklist and detect duplicates or errors |
| Your IP address, browser type, and timestamps when you access the portal | For security logging and fraud prevention |
| Electronic signature records | To record your agreement to our engagement terms |

**We do not collect** your Social Security Number, Employer Identification Number, bank account numbers, routing numbers, payment card numbers, portal passwords, 2FA codes, or any similar credentials. Our intake form is designed to block these — if you accidentally include them in an uploaded document, our system will flag the document for review and will not log the sensitive values themselves.

---

### How We Use Your Information

We use the information you share with us to:

- Conduct the neighboring rights audit you have engaged us to perform
- Communicate with you about your intake status and audit findings
- Manage your engagement, including reminders when required items are outstanding
- Comply with our legal and contractual obligations
- Maintain records of the services we have provided

**We do not sell your personal information.** We do not use your information for advertising. We do not share your financial documents with other MusiGod clients.

---

### Who We Share It With

We use a small number of technology services to operate the MusiGod platform. These services process your data on our behalf and are contractually required to protect it:

| Service | What they receive | Where |
|---------|------------------|-------|
| **Supabase** (database and file storage) | Your intake data and uploaded documents | United States (AWS us-east-1) |
| **Vercel** (our web hosting and API platform) | Request and response data passing through our API | United States |
| **Resend** (email delivery) | Your email address and the content of emails we send you — never your documents or signed URLs | United States |
| **n8n** (workflow automation for reminders) | Your engagement ID and message type — not your document content | United States (Render.com) |
| **Sentry** (error monitoring) | Technical error information — our policy prohibits personal information in error payloads | United States |

We may share your information if required by law, court order, or regulatory process. If that happens, we will notify you unless we are legally prohibited from doing so.

### Your Uploaded Documents

Your documents are stored in a private, access-controlled storage system. They are:

- **Never accessible via a public or predictable URL.** Access requires a short-lived authenticated link that expires within 5 minutes.
- **Bound to your engagement only.** MusiGod operators working on other clients' engagements cannot access your documents.
- **Scanned automatically** for accidentally included sensitive data. If sensitive data is detected in a document you upload, the document is quarantined for operator review; the sensitive values are never logged.
- **Never sent as email attachments** and never included in reminder emails.

---

- **Opt out** of the sale of your personal information (we do not sell personal information)
- **Limit** our use of sensitive personal information
- **Lodge a complaint** with your state's privacy regulator or the relevant supervisory authority
