# MusiGod Portfolio Rights Integrity Diagnostic

## Commercial offer

MusiGod analyzes an authorized music-rights portfolio and delivers an evidence-backed view of metadata integrity, ownership conflicts, royalty-statement exceptions, registration gaps and prioritized recovery opportunities.

**Fixed diagnostic fee:** $7,500, payable at engagement.

**Included scope:** Up to 1,000 musical works and/or sound recordings, up to three primary structured source datasets, one controlled analysis cycle, one Rights Recovery Report and one 60-minute executive findings review.

**Delivery target:** Ten business days after MusiGod confirms receipt of complete, readable and authorized source material.

## Ideal buyer

- Independent music publisher
- Record label or catalog owner
- Catalog-acquisition or private-credit fund
- Artist or producer management company
- Business manager
- Legacy artist estate
- Entertainment attorney coordinating a catalog review

## Customer deliverables

1. Portfolio coverage and source inventory
2. Cross-source asset matching and unmatched-record analysis
3. Missing ISRC, ISWC, IPI, publisher and recording-party findings where supported by the supplied fields
4. Duplicate and conflicting ownership-claim findings
5. Royalty-statement reconciliation exceptions
6. Prioritized recovery-opportunity register
7. Confidence, evidence, assumptions and provenance for material findings
8. Legal-review flags and recommended next actions
9. Client-facing Rights Recovery Report
10. Sixty-minute executive findings presentation

## Required source material

- Current catalog metadata export
- Ownership, split or acquisition schedule
- One relevant royalty statement or payment export
- Available registration exports or identifier records
- Source descriptions and reporting periods
- Named customer contact authorized to approve the analysis
- Signed data authorization and diagnostic agreement

MusiGod will not analyze real customer data until authorization and permitted-use scope are documented.

## Exclusions

- Legal opinions or final ownership determinations
- Audit, tax or accounting opinions
- Guaranteed recovery values or payment promises
- External registrations, corrections, claims or counterparty submissions
- Litigation, negotiation or dispute representation
- Manual review beyond the included portfolio and source limits
- API integrations, recurring monitoring or historical statement reconstruction unless separately contracted

## Commercial conversion paths

- **Retroactive recovery engagement:** 15% of amounts actually recovered, subject to a separate agreement and customer authorization.
- **Catalog registration and administration:** priced separately according to catalog size, territories and administration scope.
- **Portfolio monitoring:** recurring monthly or annual engagement for new statements, ownership changes and unresolved exceptions.
- **Expanded enterprise audit:** separately scoped when the portfolio exceeds 1,000 assets, three datasets or the included review cycle.

## Acceptance and change control

The diagnostic begins after payment, signature and data-completeness confirmation. Materially incomplete or malformed sources pause the delivery clock. Additional sources, assets or review cycles require a written change order. Findings identify data conditions and potential actions; they do not establish legal title or an amount owed unless authoritative evidence and counterparty confirmation support that conclusion.
