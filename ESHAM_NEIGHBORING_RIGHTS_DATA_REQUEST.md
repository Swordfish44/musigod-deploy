# Esham — Neighboring Rights Recovery Audit: Data Request

**Prepared by:** MusiGod  
**Date:** 2026-07-29  
**Purpose:** To conduct a neighboring-rights recovery audit, determine what sound-recording performance royalties may be owed to Esham and/or Reel Life Productions, and initiate any authorized recovery workflow.

Neighboring rights are distinct from publishing royalties. They compensate featured performers and sound-recording rightsholders when recordings are broadcast, publicly performed, or digitally transmitted. SoundExchange administers US digital performance royalties; PPL administers UK performance royalties; equivalent organizations exist in most territories.

## Section 1 — Mandatory Items

These items are required before any analysis can produce a defensible result.

### 1A. SoundExchange Direct Catalog Export

- Format: CSV or Excel
- Source: Log into SoundExchange Direct → Catalog → Export All
- Fields needed: ISRC, title, artist name, album title, release year
- Coverage: Full catalog — do not filter by date or release

### 1B. SoundExchange Payment Statements — All Available Years

- Format: PDF or CSV (prefer CSV for processing)
- Source: SoundExchange Direct → Statements
- Coverage: Every available year. Older statements back to account registration.
- Fields needed per line item: ISRC, title, payment period, gross royalties, net royalties, paid amount, held amount, adjustment amount, currency, territory/distribution type, claimant type (featured performer vs. rightsholder)

### 1C. SoundExchange Adjustment and Reversal Notices

- Any notices of payment adjustments, reversals, reissued payments, or disputed line items
- Coverage: All available history

### 1D. Master Ownership / Rightsholder Documentation

At least one of the following per release (or for the catalog as a whole):
- Master recording ownership agreements or assignments
- Distribution agreements identifying the master-recording rightsholder
- Label agreements
- Self-distribution or artist-owned-masters declarations (written statement confirming ownership)

*MusiGod will not assume master ownership based on artist name, PRO registration, or uploading the recording.*

### 1E. Featured Performer Confirmation

- A statement confirming that Esham is the featured performer on the subject recordings
- If any tracks feature guest artists who are the primary/featured performer on that recording, identify them

## Section 2 — Highly Recommended

These items substantially increase the value and accuracy of the audit.

### 2A. SoundExchange Associated Recordings and Search-and-Claim Results

- Format: CSV or PDF
- Source: SoundExchange Direct → Search and Claim
- Purpose: Identify unclaimed royalties matching Esham's ISRC or title profile

### 2B. SoundExchange Unmatched / Unidentified Recordings Report

- Format: CSV
- Any export showing recordings SoundExchange has usage data for but has not matched to a registered recording
- This is where unclaimed royalties accumulate when ISRCs or registrations are missing

### 2C. International CMO Statements (PPL, SCPP, GVL, etc.)

For each international territory where Esham's music has been distributed:
- CMO name and member ID (if any)
- Statement CSV or PDF for all available periods
- Mandate on file: Y/N; if yes, provide mandate reference

### 2D. Distributor and Label Royalty Statements

- Statements from DistroKid, TuneCore, CD Baby, or any label/distributor that distributed Esham's recordings
- These may reveal ISRCs, release dates, and platform-level usage not visible in CMO statements

### 2E. Existing Mandates on File at Any CMO

- Copies of any mandate, authority, or associated-artist agreement submitted to SoundExchange, PPL, or any other CMO
- Effective dates, territory scope, and mandate type for each
