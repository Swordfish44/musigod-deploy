# Artist Export Center Guide

**Implementation:** `lib/export-center.js`  
**Guide version:** export-center-v1  
**Purpose:** Instructions for artists on how to export their own data and upload it securely to MusiGod

> **Important:** MusiGod will never ask for your portal password, login credentials, SSN, EIN, routing number, account number, or card number. Export the files yourself and upload them through the MusiGod secure portal.

---

## Required Documents

### 1. SoundExchange Associated Recordings

**Why it's needed:** Identifies which recordings SoundExchange currently associates with your account. Essential for matching your catalog against your SoundExchange registration status and finding unregistered recordings.

**Expected format:** CSV or XLSX  
**Date coverage:** All available — export the full catalog, not a date-filtered subset.

**How to export:**
1. Log into SoundExchange Direct (SXDirect) at soundexchange.com.
2. Navigate to your catalog or recordings section.
3. Use the export or download option to save the full associated recordings list as CSV or Excel.
4. Do not share your login credentials — export the file yourself, save it to your computer, and upload it here.

**Do not email this file.** Download it locally and upload through the MusiGod secure portal only.

---

### 2. SoundExchange Search-and-Claim / Unclaimed Recordings

**Why it's needed:** SoundExchange may hold royalties for recordings not yet matched to your account. This export identifies unclaimed funds that may belong to you based on your performer identity.

**Expected format:** CSV or XLSX  
**Date coverage:** All available.

**How to export:**
1. Log into SoundExchange Direct.
2. Navigate to the search-and-claim or unclaimed recordings tool.
3. Search for your artist name and all known aliases and spelling variants.
4. Export any unclaimed or unmatched results as CSV or Excel.

**Do not email royalty or payment data.** Upload through the MusiGod secure portal only.

---

### 3. SoundExchange Payment Statements (All Years)

**Why it's needed:** Required to calculate what has already been paid, identify gaps, detect underpayments, and establish the baseline for the audit. Without complete payment history the audit cannot determine what is outstanding.

**Expected format:** CSV, XLSX, or PDF  
**Date coverage:** All available years. Request at minimum 5 years back, or as far as your SoundExchange account shows.

**How to export:**
1. Log into SoundExchange Direct.
2. Navigate to your statements or payment history section.
3. Export all available years. If the portal does not offer a combined export, download each year separately.
4. Include both digital performance and non-interactive streaming statements if shown separately.

**Payment statements contain private financial data.** Never email them. Upload through the MusiGod secure portal only.

---

### 4. Distributor Statements

**Why it's needed:** Corroborates master ownership and documents ISRC assignments. Helps verify which recordings your distributor has registered on your behalf and which ISRCs they assigned.

**Expected format:** CSV, XLSX, or PDF  
**Date coverage:** All available years.

**How to export:**
1. Log into your distributor portal (DistroKid, CD Baby, TuneCore, AWAL, or other).
2. Navigate to earnings, statements, or catalog.
3. Export all available statement history.
4. If your distributor offers a separate ISRC export, include that as a separate upload.
5. Do not share your distributor login credentials — export the files yourself.

**Do not email statements.** Export them yourself and upload through MusiGod.

---

### 5. Master Ownership and Exclusive License Evidence

**Why it's needed:** Neighboring rights require proof of master ownership or an exclusive license to master recordings. Without this no claim can be authorized regardless of other evidence.

**Expected format:** PDF or DOCX  
**Date coverage:** All periods during which you owned or exclusively licensed the masters.

**How to export:**
1. Locate recording agreements, work-for-hire agreements, or exclusive license agreements.
2. For self-funded / self-produced recordings: provide any business records confirming you produced and funded the recording (studio invoices, engineer contracts, session pay records).
3. Business entity formation documents may support master ownership where an LLC or corporation holds the rights.
4. If documents contain full SSN, EIN, bank account numbers, or routing numbers: redact those fields before uploading, or contact MusiGod for secure alternatives.

**Redact sensitive personal identifiers before uploading where possible.** Never email ownership documents.

---

## Optional Documents (Provide If Available)

### SoundExchange Adjustments and Reversals
Adjustment records change your net received total. Export from the adjustments or reversals section of SoundExchange Direct if available, or look for adjustment line items in your regular statements.

### Label Statements
Required if any recordings were released under a label deal. Contact the label accounting department for statement exports.

### PPL and International CMO Statements
If you have a PPL (UK) or other international CMO account, export your statement history. Contact other CMOs directly for exports.

### Existing International Collection Mandates
Locate signed mandate or appointment agreements with any CMO. These determine which territories have collection already in place.

---

## What MusiGod Will NOT Ask For

- Your SoundExchange Direct password or login
- Your distributor portal password
- Your label portal password
- Your Social Security Number (SSN)
- Your Employer Identification Number (EIN)
- Your bank account or routing number
- Your credit or debit card number
- Any tax form containing full personal identifiers

If anyone claiming to be MusiGod asks for these, do not provide them and contact MusiGod support immediately.
